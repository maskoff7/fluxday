# Product backlog

Candidates for later milestones, intentionally excluded from the offline MVP:

1. Signed and notarized universal macOS release with Apple Developer credentials.
2. Optional end-to-end encrypted iCloud sync.
3. Multi-currency plans with explicit exchange-rate assumptions.
4. Local notifications before forecast cash gaps.
5. CSV import mapping for banks and spreadsheets.
6. Windows packaging and platform QA.
7. Recurring-operation exceptions for one changed occurrence.
8. Multiple cash accounts and transfers.
9. Mobile companion with shared local-first data format.
10. Accessibility audit with VoiceOver users.

Bank integrations, shared accounts, AI forecasting, subscriptions and telemetry remain out of scope until there is validated demand.
